The challenge

Why paper permits fail when the work gets complex

Permit to work failures sit behind many major process incidents, from Piper Alpha onward. The common thread is not missing paperwork but missing information at the moment a permit is signed.

Isolations that nobody can see

Paper permits rarely show which energy sources are locked out, by whom and under which other permits, so isolations get removed while work is still open.

Clashing jobs in the same area

Hot work next to a line break or a confined space entry under a crane lift goes unnoticed when permits sit in separate books at separate control rooms.

Stale gas tests and expired permits

Gas readings taken at 7 a.m. still cover work at 3 p.m., and permits are extended verbally without re-validation or shift handover records.

Capabilities

A permit system that checks the job before anyone signs

Every permit carries its own risk assessment, isolations, gas tests and approvals, and the system blocks issue until each precondition is met.

Configurable permit types and forms

Set up hot work, cold work, confined space, height, electrical, excavation, radiography and lifting permits with your own checklists, PPE requirements, validity periods and mandatory fields.

Isolation and LOTO linkage

Attach electrical, mechanical and process isolation points to a permit, record lock and tag numbers, and prevent de-isolation while any linked permit remains open.

SIMOPS conflict checks

Permits are mapped to areas and equipment tags, and the system flags incompatible jobs running at the same time and place before approval, not after an incident.

Gas testing records

Log O2, LEL, H2S and CO readings with instrument ID and calibration date, set re-test intervals, and suspend a permit automatically when a reading is out of range or overdue.

Multi-level digital approvals

Route permits through issuer, area authority, performing authority and isolating authority with mobile e-signatures, time stamps and competency checks on each signatory.

QR permit boards and site display

Print a QR code for each live permit at the worksite so anyone can scan to verify status, conditions and expiry, and show every live permit on a control room board or plot plan.

Permit types covered

Each permit type ships with a starter checklist based on common industry practice, and every field, approver and validity rule can be changed to match your procedure.

Hot work

Process

How a digital permit moves through its lifecycle

Request and plan

The performing authority raises a permit from the mobile app or desktop, selects the area and equipment, and attaches the JSA or task risk assessment.

Isolate and test

Isolating authorities apply and record isolations, and gas testers log readings, while the system runs SIMOPS checks against other live permits in the area.

Approve and issue

Each authority signs off on mobile, the permit goes live with a QR code at the worksite, and validity, re-tests and shift handovers are tracked in real time.

Suspend, close and hand back

Work is closed out with a site inspection, isolations are removed in sequence, the equipment is handed back to operations and the full record is archived for audit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what buyers ask before choosing a solution.

What is permit to work software?

Permit to work software is a digital system that controls high-risk, non-routine work by issuing, approving, monitoring and closing permits electronically, with linked risk assessments, isolations and gas test records.

How does the software link permits to lockout/tagout?

Isolation points are recorded against each permit with lock and tag numbers. One isolation can cover several permits, and the system will not allow it to be removed until every linked permit is closed or suspended.

How do SIMOPS conflict checks work?

Each permit is tied to an area, unit or equipment tag. When a new permit is requested, the system compares it against live permits nearby and flags combinations your rules define as incompatible, such as hot work near a line break or a lift over an occupied confined space.

Can permits be approved on a phone without network coverage?

Yes. The Android and iOS apps support offline capture of permit details, checklists and gas readings, and sync approvals as soon as a connection is available. Sites can choose which steps require online verification.

What happens at shift change?

Outgoing and incoming permit holders complete a digital handover that records the status of work, isolations and gas tests, and the permit cannot continue on the next shift until the handover is signed.

Can contractors raise their own permits?

Yes. Contractor supervisors can request permits through the app, and the system checks their company approval and individual competency records before the permit reaches the issuing authority.
