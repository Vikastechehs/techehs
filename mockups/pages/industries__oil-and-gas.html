The challenge

Why oil and gas safety needs more than a general EHS tool

Oil and gas operations combine major accident hazards, flammable and toxic inventories and large contractor workforces, often at sites hours from the nearest office or hospital.

Process safety and SIMOPS

Loss of containment, hot work in classified hazardous areas and simultaneous drilling, production and maintenance require permits, isolations and MOC to be checked against each other, not managed in silos.

H2S and toxic exposure

Sour gas fields and refinery units need gas test records, personal monitor data, H2S training status and rescue readiness verified before people enter the work area.

Turnarounds and remote sites

During shutdowns contractor headcount can multiply overnight, while offshore and desert sites run with little connectivity, making paper inductions, permits and competency checks the weak point.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what buyers ask before choosing a solution.

Does TECH EHS support OSHA PSM compliance?

Yes. Modules for MOC, PSSR, incident investigation, operating procedures, contractor management, training and audits map to the elements of OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119 and similar process safety regulations.

Can process safety events be classified under API RP 754?

Yes. Loss of primary containment events are classified as Tier 1 to Tier 4 using API RP 754 and IOGP 456 thresholds for material release, consequence and location, and reported as rates by asset.

How does the system handle SIMOPS?

Permits and planned activities are tied to areas or equipment, and configurable rules flag incompatible combinations such as hot work during well intervention or crane lifts over pressurized lines, before approval.

Does the mobile app work offshore without connectivity?

Yes. The Android and iOS apps work fully offline for permits, inspections, observations and incidents, and synchronize when satellite or Wi-Fi connectivity is available.

Can it manage H2S safety?

Yes. Gas test records, H2S training and fit test status, monitor assignments and rescue team readiness can be required as preconditions for permits in sour service areas.

Do you provide oil and gas training content too?

Yes. TECH EHS produces HSE e-learning and animated safety videos on topics such as H2S awareness, permit to work, confined space and incident recreation, in multiple languages.
