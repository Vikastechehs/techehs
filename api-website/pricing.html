Pricing questions

What happens when I run out of credits?

Requests return HTTP 402 until you upgrade or buy a top-up pack. Nothing is charged automatically unless you enable auto top-up.

Can I buy credits without a subscription?

Yes. One-off packs start at $49 for 12,000 credits and never expire.

Do you offer refunds?

If you haven't used any credits from a new subscription, you can request a full refund within 7 days.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards. Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer against an invoice.
