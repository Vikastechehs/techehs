The challenge

Why classroom slides and long manuals don't change behavior

Most frontline safety communication still relies on text-heavy procedures and presentations delivered in one language. Workers sign attendance sheets, but the message rarely reaches the task.

Multilingual, mixed-literacy workforces

Contract crews on a single site may speak five or more languages, and a written procedure in English protects few of them.

Hazards you cannot film

Toxic releases, arc flash, falls and confined space engulfment cannot be shown safely with a camera, so they are explained in words instead of shown.

Lessons from incidents fade fast

Investigation reports are filed and forgotten, and the same failure repeats at another site because nobody saw how it actually happened.

Process

Our production process

Brief and script

We review your procedures, incident reports and site photos, then an EHS scriptwriter drafts the narration and learning objectives for your approval.

Storyboard and style frames

Each scene is sketched with characters, equipment and camera angles, and you sign off the look before any animation starts.

Animation and voiceover

Our 2D and 3D artists build the scenes and recreate your site, while voice artists record the narration in each language you need.

Review, revise and deliver

You review a full draft with timestamped comments, we apply revisions, and final files are delivered as MP4, SCORM or both.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what buyers ask before choosing a solution.

What are animated safety videos?

They are short 2D or 3D animated films that explain hazards, procedures, site rules or incidents visually, so workers can see what to do and why without relying on written text.

Should we choose 2D or 3D animation?

2D suits rules, concepts and behavioral messages and is faster to produce. 3D suits equipment, plant layouts and tasks where workers need to recognize the real environment. Many projects mix both.

How long does a typical safety animation take to produce?

A 2 to 4 minute video usually takes 3 to 6 weeks* from approved brief to final delivery, depending on 3D complexity, number of languages and review cycles.

Can you recreate our actual site and equipment?

Yes. We build scenes from your photos, drawings, P&IDs or CAD models, and match your PPE, signage, uniforms and color codes.

Which languages do you offer for voiceover?

We offer professional voiceover and subtitles in 40+ languages*, including English, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Tagalog, Bengali, Tamil and Urdu.

Can the videos be tracked in our LMS?

Yes. Videos can be packaged as SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 with optional quizzes, or delivered through the TECH EHS e-learning and induction platforms.
