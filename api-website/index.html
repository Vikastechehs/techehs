FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does billing work?

Every successful request costs credits based on the endpoint (see the rate table). Failed requests, timeouts and empty results are never charged. Buy a monthly plan or a one-off credit pack.

Do I need to provide cookies or an account?

No. You only need your Techehs API key. We never ask for your personal credentials.

Is the data real-time?

Yes. Each request fetches the latest public data at the time of the call. We don't serve results from a stale database.

What are the rate limits?

Concurrency depends on your plan: 2 on Free, 5 on Starter, 15 on Growth, 50 on Business, and custom on Enterprise. See rate limits.

Do unused credits roll over?

On monthly plans, unused credits roll over for one additional month. Credit packs never expire.

Is this compliant with data protection laws?

We only collect publicly available information. You are responsible for using the data in line with GDPR, CCPA and other laws that apply to you. Read our Acceptable Use Policy.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard in one click. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.
