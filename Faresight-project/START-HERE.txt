FARESIGHT (working name) - PROJECT PACK
Updated 31 Oct 2026

WHAT IT IS
A free flight-deal site and app. We only show return fares that are bookable now and at
least 25% below the route's real normal price, re-checked every 6 hours. It launches from
India first, then other countries. It earns from booking commissions (never shown on the site).
Hotels come later.

WHERE WE ARE
- Research and competitor audit: done (only 26 of 90 rival deals were real and bookable)
- Flight engine: built; real-fare test passed (48 of 50 live searches, 480 credits)
- Website design: done, flights only (home, deal, route, alerts, mobile home, mobile deal)
- Name, domain, company, affiliate accounts: not done yet
- Target: soft launch Wed 4 Nov 2026 to 50-100 people

WE LAUNCH WITH 5 SERVICES
1. Verified flight deals (25%+ below normal, re-checked every 6 hours, mistake-fare label)
2. City alerts on Telegram and WhatsApp channel
3. Every working date, with a price calendar
4. Explore from your airport, by budget or region
5. Honest deal endings (ended deals stay up with the reason)
Weeks 2-4: route guides, "Is this a good price?" checker, route watch, email alerts, Android app.

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
1-blueprint\Faresight-Blueprint.pdf
    The full plan: launch plan, product, revenue, roadmap, growth, budget, risks,
    website and app research, protection from scrapers.
2-website-design\
    clickable-preview\index.html  -> open in a browser; pages link to each other
    screenshots\                   -> one image per page
    Faresight-website-design.pdf   -> all pages in one PDF
3-flight-deal-engine\
    The working engine (Windows). Needs Node.js 22.18 or newer.
    1. setup.bat      installs it and creates the database
    2. test-real.bat  50 real searches (about 500 Scrape.do credits)
    3. start.bat      runs everything; website at http://localhost:3000, admin at /admin
    4. status.bat     shows prices stored, deals and credits used
    Put your Scrape.do key in .env (created by setup.bat). Never share .env.
