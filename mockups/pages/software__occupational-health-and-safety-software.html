The challenge

Why fragmented OHS data puts workers and certifications at risk

Most safety teams still run their OHS program across email, Excel trackers and paper binders. The result is lagging data, missed actions and audit findings that could have been prevented.

Hazards reported but never closed

Without an owner, due date and escalation, corrective actions sit in inboxes until the same hazard causes an injury.

No single view of risk

Incident, inspection and health data live in different files, so leaders cannot see which sites, tasks or contractors carry the most risk.

Audit preparation takes weeks

Compiling evidence for ISO 45001 surveillance audits or regulator visits means hunting through folders instead of pulling a report.

Capabilities

Everything an OHS management system needs, connected

Start with the modules you need today and switch on more as your program matures. Every module shares the same users, locations, risk matrix and action tracker.

Incident and near miss reporting

Capture injuries, illnesses, property damage and near misses in under two minutes from a phone, with photos, GPS and voice notes, then route them for investigation and root cause analysis.

Hazard identification and risk assessment

Build HIRA and JSA registers using your own risk matrix, link controls to the hierarchy of controls, and trigger reviews when tasks, equipment or legislation change.

Inspections and audits

Run scheduled and ad hoc checklists with scoring, photo evidence and automatic action creation for every failed item.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what buyers ask before choosing a solution.

What is occupational health and safety software?

It is a digital system for managing the core processes of an OHS program, including incident reporting, risk assessment, inspections, permits, training and health surveillance, with a shared action tracker and reporting layer.

Does TECH EHS support ISO 45001 certification?

Yes. Workflows map to ISO 45001:2018 clauses such as hazard identification (6.1.2), incident investigation (10.2) and performance evaluation (9.1), and evidence can be exported for certification and surveillance audits.

Can we start with a few modules and add more later?

Yes. Most clients start with incident management, inspections and permits, then add modules such as contractor management, training or occupational health without migrating data.

Does the mobile app work without internet?

Yes. The Android and iOS apps capture reports, inspections and photos offline and sync automatically when a connection is available.

Can it be deployed on our own servers?

TECH EHS is available as cloud SaaS or on-premise, and supports Azure AD single sign-on for enterprise identity management.

How does it handle OSHA recordkeeping?

Incidents can be classified against OSHA recordability criteria and summarized in OSHA 300, 300A and 301 style logs, alongside local formats such as RIDDOR.
